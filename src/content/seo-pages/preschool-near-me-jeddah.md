## What is Preschool? Ages, Goals & Benefits

### Understanding Preschool Education

**Age Range**: Typically 2-5 years old  
**Main Goal**: School readiness through play-based learning  
**Duration**: Usually 3-4 hours/day (half-day) or 6-7 hours (full-day)

### Preschool vs. Daycare vs. Kindergarten

Many parents confuse these terms. Here's the clarity you need:

| Program | Age | Primary Focus | Structure |
|---------|-----|---------------|-----------|
| **Daycare** | 0-3 years | Care & supervision | Flexible, play-oriented |
| **Preschool** | 2-5 years | Early learning | Semi-structured curriculum |
| **Kindergarten** | 4-6 years | Academic preparation | Structured, school-like |

**Key difference**: Preschool balances play and learning - not just supervision (daycare) and not too academic (kindergarten).

### Key Differences

| Aspect | Montessori Preschool | Traditional Preschool |
|--------|---------------------|----------------------|
| **Teacher Role** | Guide/observer | Instructor |
| **Learning** | Self-directed | Teacher-directed |
| **Materials** | Specific Montessori tools | Standard toys/books |
| **Schedule** | Flexible work cycles | Fixed time blocks |
| **Assessment** | Observation | Tests/reports |
| **Age Groups** | Mixed (2-6) | Same age |

- You want more direct instruction

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What age should my child start preschool?
**A**: Most children are ready between 2.5-3 years. Signs of readiness include: basic communication, interest in other children, and can handle separation for short periods.

### Q: Is preschool necessary or can I teach at home?
**A**: While home teaching is valuable, preschool offers:
- Socialization with peers
- Learning in group settings
- Professional early childhood education
- Exposure to structure and routines
- Preparation for formal schooling

### Q: My child doesn't speak English/Arabic yet. Will they be okay?
**A**: Absolutely! Young children are natural language learners. Most catch up within 3-6 months through immersion.

### Q: What if my child has behavioral issues?
**A**: We work with families on common challenges (hitting, biting, tantrums). If specialized support is needed, we'll guide you to appropriate resources.

### Q: Do you give homework?
**A**: No formal homework for preschoolers. We may suggest activities to do as a family, but nothing stressful.

### Q: Can I visit during the day?
**A**: Yes! We have an open-door policy. Schedule visits during specified hours to minimize disruption.

### Q: What's your sick policy?
**A**: Children must be fever-free for 24 hours before returning. We ask parents to keep children home if they're contagious.

### Q: How do you handle discipline?
**A**: Positive discipline focused on teaching, not punishing. Natural consequences, redirection, and respectful communication.

### Q: Will Montessori preschool prepare my child for traditional school?
**A**: Yes! Montessori students typically excel in traditional schools due to strong foundations in reading, math, and self-regulation.
