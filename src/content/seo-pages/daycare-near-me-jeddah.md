## Montessori vs. Traditional Daycare: What's the Difference?

| Aspect | Montessori Daycare | Traditional Daycare |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Learning Style** | Child-directed | Teacher-directed |
| **Class Structure** | Mixed ages | Same age groups |
| **Materials** | Montessori apparatus | Standard toys |
| **Focus** | Independence | Group activities |
| **Assessment** | Observation-based | Testing-based |
| **Philosophy** | "Follow the child" | Curriculum-driven |

### Which is Better for Your Child?
**Choose Montessori if** your child:
- Is curious and likes to explore
- Benefits from structure with freedom
- Needs to develop independence
- Learns at their own pace

**Choose Traditional if** your child:
- Thrives in group settings
- Responds well to direct instruction
- Needs more social interaction focus
