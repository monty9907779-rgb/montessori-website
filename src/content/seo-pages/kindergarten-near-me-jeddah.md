## What is Kindergarten? Understanding KG in Saudi Arabia

### Age Groups & Levels

**KG1 (Pre-KG)**
- Age: 3-4 years
- Focus: Social skills, basic Arabic/English, motor development
- Duration: Half-day or full-day options

**KG2**
- Age: 4-5 years
- Focus: Pre-reading, numbers, creative expression
- Preparation: Building readiness for KG3

**KG3 (Pre-Grade 1)**
- Age: 5-6 years
- Focus: Reading readiness, math concepts, critical thinking
- Outcome: Ready for primary school

### Kindergarten vs. Nursery vs. Preschool

Many parents confuse these terms. Here's the clear difference:

| Term | Age Range | Focus | Goal |
|------|-----------|-------|------|
| **Nursery** | 0-3 years | Basic care + stimulation | Development milestones |
| **Preschool** | 2-4 years | Play-based learning | Social skills |
| **Kindergarten** | 3-6 years | Academic preparation | School readiness |

**In Saudi Arabia**, "kindergarten" and "روضة" often mean the same thing - formal early education before Grade 1.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I know if my child is ready for kindergarten?
**A**: Signs of readiness include: separating from parents for short periods, basic self-care (toilet, washing hands), following simple instructions, and showing interest in learning activities. We can assess during a visit.

### Q: What if my child doesn't know Arabic or English yet?
**A**: No problem! Children are amazing language learners. Our bilingual teachers use immersion methods that work naturally. Most children catch up within 3-6 months.

### Q: Will Montessori kindergarten prepare my child for traditional primary school?
**A**: Absolutely. Montessori KG students typically enter Grade 1 ahead of peers in reading, math, and self-regulation. They adapt quickly to any school environment.

### Q: Can my child start mid-year?
**A**: Yes, we accept enrollments year-round when spaces are available. Montessori's individualized approach makes transitions easier.

### Q: Do you follow the Saudi Ministry curriculum?
**A**: We exceed Ministry standards using the Montessori method. Our students learn all required concepts plus much more, through hands-on exploration rather than rote memorization.

### Q: What happens if my child has behavioral challenges?
**A**: Montessori environments naturally support positive behavior through independence, choice, and respect. We work closely with families. If specialized support is needed, we'll guide you to appropriate resources.

### Q: Is there homework?
**A**: No traditional homework for KG. We may suggest family activities or reading together, but nothing stressful. Childhood is for play and exploration!

### Q: How do you handle discipline?
**A**: We use positive discipline focused on teaching, not punishing. Natural consequences, respectful communication, and problem-solving are our tools.
