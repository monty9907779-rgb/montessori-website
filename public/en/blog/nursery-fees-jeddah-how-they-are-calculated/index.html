Nursery Fees in Jeddah: How They Are Calculated and What to Ask

Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr Kindergarten team ·21 October 2026·6 min read

Nursery fees in Jeddah are not a single fixed price. They are usually calculated from the child's age, the number of days per week, the hours attended each day and a small set of one-off items such as registration, and what is included varies from school to school. The fairest way to compare nurseries is to ask each one for the same breakdown and check what the fee actually covers. At Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr in Al Faisaliyyah, fees depend on the child's age, the number of days and the hours attended, and the exact figure is confirmed by phone or during a visit.

Why there is no single "nursery fee" in Jeddah

Parents often search for how much nursery costs in Jeddah and expect one answer. In practice, a daycare with long hours, a morning-only kindergarten and a Montessori nursery with trained guides and a full set of materials are offering different services, and their fees reflect that. Even within one school, two families can pay different amounts because their children attend on different days or for different hours. Understanding how the fee is built up lets you compare like with like. Our overviews of daycare in Jeddah and kindergarten in Jeddah explain how these types of setting differ.

The main factors that set the fee

  1. The child's age. Younger children need more adults per child, more help with toileting, feeding and rest, and more one-to-one attention, so the youngest groups are usually the most expensive. Older kindergarten groups with larger class sizes are typically less. If you are enrolling a two-year-old, read our guide to nursery for a 2-year-old in Jeddah first.
  2. The number of days per week. Most nurseries offer a full week and one or two part-time patterns. Fewer days means a lower fee, but the cost per day is often higher.
  3. The programme or stage. Schools that run several stages, for example Pre-KG (2–3), KG1 (3–4) and KG2 (4–5), may set a different fee for each, because the ratios and materials change as children grow.
  4. How you pay. Some nurseries quote per month, some per term and some per year. Paying for a longer period up front is often a little cheaper, but you should ask what happens if your child leaves early or you move house.

One-off and occasional items

Beyond the recurring fee, most nurseries in Jeddah have a short list of items that appear once a year or once at the start. Together they can change the real cost noticeably, so ask about every one of them before you sign.

  • Registration or application fee, usually paid once when the child joins.
  • Refundable deposit, returned when the child leaves, usually with notice conditions.
  • Uniform, if the school uses one, including PE or summer versions.
  • Materials or resource fee, sometimes bundled, sometimes separate.
  • Meals and snacks, which may be included, optional or brought from home.
  • Trips, events and celebrations through the year.
  • Late collection charges for pick-ups after the agreed time.
  • Summer programme, normally priced separately from the school year.

What is usually included

In most Jeddah nurseries the core fee covers the teaching programme, classroom materials, supervision throughout the agreed hours and standard parent communication such as a daily note or app updates. Snacks, meals, transport, uniform and extended hours most often sit outside it. Never assume; two schools can quote the same headline figure while one includes meals and the other does not.

Hidden-cost checklist

  • Is the registration fee paid once, or again every year?
  • Is the deposit refundable, and what notice period applies?
  • Are fees charged for public holidays and school breaks?
  • What happens to the fee if my child is ill for a long stretch?
  • Is there a charge for changing from part-time to full-time, or the reverse?
  • Are siblings treated differently, and how?
  • Does the fee rise during the year, and how much notice is given?
  • Which payment methods are accepted, and are instalments possible?

Questions to ask before you enrol

  1. Can you give me a written breakdown for my child's age, on the days and hours I need?
  2. Exactly what does that figure include, and what is extra?
  3. When is payment due, and what are the options?
  4. What is your withdrawal and refund policy?
  5. Is hourly or flexible care available, and how is it charged?

Ask every school the same questions and write the answers down. It removes most of the surprises families complain about later.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does nursery cost in Jeddah?

There is no single figure. Fees are set by the child's age, the number of days per week, the hours attended each day and a few one-off items such as registration. Ask each nursery for a written breakdown for your child and compare what is included.

Why do nurseries charge more for younger children?

Younger children need more adults per child and more hands-on care with toileting, feeding and rest, so staffing costs are higher. Fees usually fall as children move into the older kindergarten groups.

Are nursery fees in Jeddah paid monthly, per term or per year?

All three are common. Longer payment periods are often slightly cheaper, but ask about the refund policy if your child leaves early, and whether the registration fee is paid once or every year.
