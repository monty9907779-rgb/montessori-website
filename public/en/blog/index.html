Parent Guides & Montessori Insights

Welcome to the Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr Kindergarten blog — practical guides written for parents in Jeddah. We share what we learn every day working with children aged 3 to 5: how to choose the right nursery, the difference between Pre-KG, KG and kindergarten programs, easing first-day separation anxiety, Montessori activities you can try at home, and everyday advice on nutrition, sleep and skill development. Every article is written in plain language by our team at Al Faisaliyyah district, Jeddah. If you have a question we have not covered yet, message us on WhatsApp and we will be happy to help.

Practical, honest guidance for families in Jeddah — choosing childcare, understanding early years, and the Montessori approach we live every day.

What you will find in these guides

Choosing childcare in a city the size of Jeddah raises the same questions for almost every family: which option suits a two-year-old, what a Montessori classroom actually looks like, how fees are calculated, and how to make the first weeks calm for a child who has never been away from home. The guides below answer those questions in plain English, drawing on our work with children aged 3 to 5 in our Al Faisaliyyah nursery.

  • Nursery in Jeddah: how to compare nurseries, the questions worth asking on a visit, and the signs of a safe, well-run setting.
  • Kindergarten in Jeddah: the official stage names (KG1 for 3 to 4 and KG2 for 4 to 5), what each year focuses on, and how school readiness is built.
  • Montessori nursery guide: the prepared environment, the role of the guide, practical life and sensorial work, and how Arabic, English and Quran fit into a Montessori morning.
  • Preschool in Jeddah: the right starting age, what a good preschool morning looks like, and the questions to ask when you visit.
  • Montessori near me in Jeddah: how to tell authentic Montessori from the label, what to look for in the classroom, and how to judge a location.
  • Arabic and English kindergarten in Jeddah: how bilingual learning works day to day, how Quran fits in, and what to ask a school about language.
  • Nursery for a 2-year-old in Jeddah: the signs of readiness, what Pre-KG offers at age 2, and how to prepare your child for the first morning.
  • Settling in: the first weeks at nursery: how to prepare your child, handle drop-off tears and what a normal settling-in period looks like.
  • Montessori vs traditional preschool in Jeddah: how the classroom, the teacher's role and the pace differ, and which approach may suit your child.
  • Montessori activities at home for ages 3 to 5: simple practical-life, sensory, language and counting activities that need no special equipment.
  • Choosing a nursery in Jeddah as an expat family: language, hours, communication and settling in, written for families new to the city.
  • The best Montessori nursery in Jeddah: how to judge: a judging framework, a visit checklist and how to read reviews sensibly, instead of a ranking.
  • Nursery fees in Jeddah: how they are calculated: age, days, hours, one-off items and a hidden-cost checklist, so you can compare schools fairly.
  • Kindergarten near me in Jeddah: how to choose: what "near me" should mean in Jeddah traffic, how to use Google Maps well, and a checklist for your three closest options.
  • Nursery school, daycare, preschool or kindergarten: the terms explained: what each word means in Saudi Arabia versus the UK and US, which fits which age, and what to search for in Jeddah.

How to use them

Start with the guide that matches your child's age, then read the Montessori guide to understand how a prepared environment differs from a traditional classroom. Each article ends with a short checklist you can take on a visit. If you would like to see our environment in person, book a visit from the English homepage or message us on WhatsApp; visits run Sunday to Thursday between 10:00 AM and 12:00 PM.
