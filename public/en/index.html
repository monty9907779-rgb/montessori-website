Our method

We prepare the room; the child leads the learning

Children learn naturally when the place around them is safe and suited to their age. So we set up the classroom with hands-on, sensory materials sized for small hands, and we let each child choose, try and repeat.

The teacher’s job is to observe, guide gently and respect each child’s pace, without comparing or rushing.

  • A prepared environmentEvery material in its place and sized for the child, inviting safe discovery.
  • Freedom within limitsChildren choose their work, and their will and responsibility grow together.
  • A guiding teacherShe observes rather than dictates, and steps in when a child needs her.
  • Respect for paceEvery child has their own speed, and we do not compare.
