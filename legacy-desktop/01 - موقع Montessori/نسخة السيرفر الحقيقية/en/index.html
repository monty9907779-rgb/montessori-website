Our Educational Approach

A child is not a vessel to fill,
but a flame to kindle

At Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr, we believe every child carries a natural power to learn when placed in the right safe and caring environment.

We prepare rich hands-on activities and give children room to choose, try and discover — growing confidence and a love of learning. Our role is to observe, gently guide, and honour each child’s rhythm.

A prepared environmentEvery material in its place, child-sized, inviting safe discovery.
Freedom within limitsChildren choose their work, growing will and responsibility together.
A guiding teacherShe observes rather than dictates, stepping in only when needed.
Respect for paceNo comparing, no rushing — every child’s speed is celebrated.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr Kindergarten, Al-Faisaliyah, Jeddah

Where is Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr Kindergarten located?
We are in the Al-Faisaliyah district of Jeddah, on Mohammed Abdulkarim Street. You are welcome to visit us or book a tour through our website.
What ages do you accept?
We welcome children aged 2 to 5 across our nursery, pre-K and kindergarten programs, plus a summer program.
What makes Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr different?
We provide a safe, carefully prepared environment, hands-on age-appropriate activities, Arabic and English learning, and strong values and character care.
What are your working hours?
We are open Sunday through Thursday, from 08:00 to 13:00.
How can I book a visit or contact you?
You can reach us on WhatsApp at +966541558173, email info@montessori-ksa.com, or book directly through our website. Fees are shared on request.
Has the kindergarten changed its name?
Yes — Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr is the new official name for the kindergarten formerly known as Montessori Nursery. Same team and same place in Al-Faisaliyah district, Jeddah.
What curriculum do you follow?
We follow a child-centered, hands-on approach that supports independence, language growth and confidence through purposeful activities.
What are the fees?
Fees depend on your child's age, the program, and the number of days per week. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the exact fees right away.
