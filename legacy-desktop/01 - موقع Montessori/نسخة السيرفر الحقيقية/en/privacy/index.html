Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-27

At Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr Kindergarten (Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr Kindergarten) in Al Faisaliyyah, Jeddah, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, why, how we protect it, and what your rights are.

Information we collect

  • Contact details: name, mobile number and email when you contact us through our website, WhatsApp, or our social media pages.
  • Message content: the enquiries you send us via WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram or our website form, used to reply to you and follow up on your request.
  • Child information at enrolment: name, date of birth and health information necessary for your child's safety and care — provided by the parent or guardian only.
  • Usage data: aggregate statistics about site visits (pages viewed, traffic source) via analytics tools such as Google Analytics; this is not used to identify you personally.

How we use information

  • To answer your enquiries and arrange introductory visits.
  • To complete enrolment and care for your child at the nursery.
  • To keep you updated about your child's day, activities and fees.
  • To improve our website and services based on general statistics.

Sharing your data

We never sell or rent your data. We only share it in these cases:

  • Technical service providers who help us operate the website and messaging systems (such as messaging platforms and analytics), and only to the extent necessary.
  • Where required by law or by an official request from a competent authority in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Data protection

Our website uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to family data is limited to authorised management and teachers only, and we retain data only as long as needed to provide our service or as required by law.

Children's privacy

Our website is directed to parents and guardians, not to children. We collect information about a child only directly from their parent or guardian, and solely for care and education purposes. Photographs of children are never published on our channels without the guardian's consent.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the site and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use it. You can disable these in your browser settings without affecting access to public content.

Your rights

  • Request access to the data we hold about you or your child.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data, within what regulations and educational obligations allow.
  • Withdraw consent for the use of your child's photographs at any time.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your data or your child's data, contact us on WhatsApp or email info@montessori-ksa.com. We will respond within a reasonable period after verifying your identity.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the last-updated date appears at the top of this page.
