Parent Guides & Montessori Insights

Welcome to the Kawkab Al-Tifl Al-Hurr Kindergarten blog — practical guides written for parents in Jeddah. We share what we learn every day working with children aged 2 to 5: how to choose the right nursery, the difference between Pre-KG, KG and kindergarten programs, easing first-day separation anxiety, Montessori activities you can try at home, and everyday advice on nutrition, sleep and skill development. Every article is written in plain language by our team at Al Faisaliyyah district, Jeddah. If you have a question we have not covered yet, message us on WhatsApp and we will be happy to help.

Practical, honest guidance for families in Jeddah — choosing childcare, understanding early years, and the Montessori approach we live every day.

We keep this page updated because many parents land here first while searching for clear answers about early childhood care in Jeddah. If you are comparing nurseries, look for the same things we talk about in our guides: a calm prepared environment, trained educators, a routine that supports independence, healthy meals, and a simple way to stay in touch with the school. Our articles are designed to help you move from a quick search to a confident decision, with examples you can use during a visit and questions you can ask before enrollment.

You will also find bilingual resources that make the Montessori method easier to understand in both Arabic and English. We write for real family decisions, not for jargon, so each article stays practical, local, and easy to scan when you are planning your next step.
